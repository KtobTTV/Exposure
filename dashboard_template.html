How to read this

  • +3 means he's in your lineups in 3 more leagues than your opponents' lineups: root for him. −3 is the reverse: root against him.
  • Tank leagues are flipped: your own players count against you, your opponents' players count for you.
  • "Team lean KC +4 · BUF −3" adds up the nets of each team's players, counted in leagues. Open the game for "Game total": everyone in it you root for, and everyone you root against.
  • Live and finished games show each player's line under his name, such as "5 rec · 72 yds · 1 TD · 14.2 pts". The points are Sleeper's standard PPR. "no ESPN stats" means ESPN has no box-score entry for him, so nothing is shown rather than a false 0.
  • Finished games show the net points swing: each player's PPR points times his net, added up over all your leagues. "Helped +40 pts" means your players, on balance, scored 40 more points for you than against you. Standard PPR, not each league's own scoring. Players Sleeper has no stats for count as 0, and the card says who.
  • Stat bars: the filled part is what he has so far and the tick is the line. A stub past the tick means he's over it. Bars only compare him to the line; swing and points tell you whether he helped. "DK" means a DraftKings line, and "proj" means a Sleeper projection (used when no DraftKings line is posted, and always for Fantasy pts).
  • The Matchups tab shows your score in every league this week, the closest first. "Down 3.2 · Proj win by 10.8" is where you stand now and where you should finish: live players count what they have plus their projection for the time left. Scoring is each league's own. "Up 3.5" is good. The count at the top says how many are going your way. In tank leagues, losing is the goal, so the pill also says "good" or "bad", e.g. "Down 1.4 · good".
  • On the Players tab, "Sort & filter" opens the ranking and filters. The strongest root-for and root-against players come first. Players who cancel out (0) sit in a collapsed "Even" group, and finished players in a collapsed group at the bottom.
  • In "Sort & filter", "Count buy-in $" adds up the buy-ins of every league where a player is for or against you. "+$320" means he's for you in $320 more of buy-ins than against you. Set the buy-ins in Settings, the gear at the top; they stay on this device only.
