How to read this
  • +3 means he's in your lineups in 3 more leagues than your opponents' lineups: root for him. −3 is the reverse: root against him.
  • Tank leagues are flipped: your own players count against you, your opponents' players count for you.
  • The chip beside each team's score (+5, −1) adds up the nets of that team's players. "Game total" adds up everyone in the game.
  • Finished games say "Went your way", "Went against you" or "Even": did the winning team's players net out in your favor? A rough guide, not your fantasy score.
  • Stat bars: the filled part is what he has so far and the tick is the DraftKings line. A stub past the tick means he's over the line, and the label says whether that helps or hurts you. "proj" means the line is a Sleeper projection (used when no DraftKings line is posted, and always for Fantasy pts, which use Sleeper's PPR scoring).
